{# `class="keyboard-help"` AND NO `role`/`aria-label`, both deliberately. The CLASS is a shipped promise: with scripting off there is no overlay, so this page is the ONLY way to read the list (NFR-4 / ODD-8), and the no-JS scenario of keyboard-shortcut-bindings finds the list by `.keyboard-help`. Dropping it here left that operator with a page the lane could not find. The ROLE stays off: `role="dialog"` on a whole document announces the page she navigated to as a dialog she can dismiss, and there is nothing behind it to go back to. The dialog container belongs to the overlay arm alone, which is why this page includes the ROWS partial and not that container. The overlay's own chrome — the scrim and the raised card — stays scoped to `#kb-overlay-root`, so this section takes no card of its own: the frame here is `.page-card`. #}
